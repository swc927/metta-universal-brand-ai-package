# Start Here

This package helps you create or review Metta communications. It does not approve or publish work for you.

## I have never used AI before

Open [`BEGINNER_PROMPTS.md`](BEGINNER_PROMPTS.md). Copy **“I do not know where to start”** and let the AI ask you one simple question at a time. You may answer **I do not know**; the AI should mark missing information instead of guessing.

## I want AI to create something

1. Open the AI tool approved for your work.
2. Follow the relevant instructions in [`PLATFORM_SETUP.md`](PLATFORM_SETUP.md).
3. Attach the current approved facts for your task.
4. Copy this prompt:

```text
Use the Metta Universal Brand Guide to prepare a [TYPE OF MATERIAL] for [AUDIENCE].

Purpose: [PURPOSE]
Required action: [ACTION]
Approved facts: [PASTE OR ATTACH]
Language: [ENGLISH / SIMPLIFIED CHINESE / BILINGUAL]
Channel: [EMAIL / POSTER / SOCIAL MEDIA / WEBSITE / REPORT / OTHER]

Do not invent missing information. Return the complete draft, facts requiring verification, accessibility and privacy checks, and the approval route before release.
```

Start with a suitable file in [`templates/`](templates/README.md) when available.

If you are unsure how to describe the task, use the ready-made email, poster, social-media, bilingual and review prompts in [`BEGINNER_PROMPTS.md`](BEGINNER_PROMPTS.md).

## I want to check existing work

Attach the material and use:

```text
Review this material against the Metta Universal Brand Guide. Do not publish or change a live system. Classify findings as Blocker, Required, Recommended or Optional. Check identity and logo, names and facts, respectful language, bilingual quality, accessibility, privacy and consent, fundraising disclosures, partner requirements and approvals.
```

## I want to use the Metta logo

- Use the supplied master asset; never redraw or retype it.
- Keep the complete bilingual composite, correct proportions and clear space.
- Minimum printed width is 20 mm unless Community Partnership approves otherwise.
- The reviewed Metta rules do **not** impose a blanket ban on bottom or footer placement. Bottom placement is acceptable when the logo remains legible, clear and consistent with the approved template.
- External use, co-branding and exceptions require Community Partnership approval.

## Before releasing anything

- Verify every date, address, fee, eligibility condition, contact, statistic and link.
- Confirm consent, confidentiality, copyright and safeguarding requirements.
- Obtain the content owner’s review.
- Obtain Community Partnership review where identity, logo, media, campaign or partner use is involved.
- A draft or AI response is not permission to publish or send.
