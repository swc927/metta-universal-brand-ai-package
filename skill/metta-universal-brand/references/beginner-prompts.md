# Beginner Prompt Guide

You do not need to know how to write an AI prompt. Choose one option below, copy the complete block, and replace anything inside `[square brackets]`. If you do not know an answer, write **I do not know**. The AI must not guess.

Before starting, upload this package or add it to the AI workspace. At minimum, provide `START_HERE.md`, `EMPLOYEE_QUICK_GUIDE.md`, `METTA_UNIVERSAL_BRAND_GUIDE.md` and the relevant approved facts.

## 1. I do not know where to start

```text
I am a Metta employee and I am new to using AI. Study the attached Metta brand package before helping me.

I need help with: [DESCRIBE IT, OR WRITE "I DO NOT KNOW"]

Guide me by asking one short, simple question at a time. Start by finding out the purpose, audience, channel, deadline, language and information source. Explain unfamiliar terms. Do not ask me for information I have already provided.

Do not invent or assume Metta facts, dates, prices, eligibility, programme details, contact information, links, translations, testimonials, logo rules or approval status. If information is missing, mark it clearly as [TO CONFIRM].

When you have enough information:
1. prepare the complete draft;
2. explain briefly which Metta guidance you followed;
3. list every missing or unverified item;
4. identify the human reviews and approvals needed; and
5. give me a final checklist before publishing, sending or printing.

Use only supplied official logo artwork. Do not publish, send, upload or change a live system. Do not say the work is approved unless I provide evidence of approval.
```

## 2. Create an email or announcement

```text
Study the attached Metta brand package. Help me draft a [EMAIL / ANNOUNCEMENT].

Audience: [WHO WILL RECEIVE IT]
Purpose: [WHY WE ARE WRITING]
Action required: [WHAT THEY SHOULD DO]
Approved facts: [PASTE OR ATTACH THEM]
Language: [ENGLISH / SIMPLIFIED CHINESE / BILINGUAL]
Deadline: [DATE, OR I DO NOT KNOW]

Ask me one question at a time for anything essential that is missing. Do not guess. Then provide the complete draft, a subject or heading, a verification list and the required approval route. Do not send it.
```

## 3. Create a poster or flyer brief

```text
Study the attached Metta brand package. Help me prepare a design brief for a [POSTER / FLYER / DIGITAL SCREEN].

Event or message: [NAME]
Audience: [WHO]
Required facts: [PASTE OR ATTACH]
Size or channel: [A4 / INSTAGRAM / SCREEN / I DO NOT KNOW]
Language: [ENGLISH / SIMPLIFIED CHINESE / BILINGUAL]
Call to action: [WHAT PEOPLE SHOULD DO]

Ask me one simple question at a time until the brief is usable. Separate confirmed facts from [TO CONFIRM] items. Include copy hierarchy, accessibility, imagery, official-logo handling, QR/link checks and final approvals. Do not invent event details or create a false beneficiary image.
```

## 4. Create a social-media post

```text
Study the attached Metta brand package. Draft a post for [FACEBOOK / INSTAGRAM / LINKEDIN / OTHER].

Purpose: [PURPOSE]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Approved facts and link: [PASTE OR ATTACH]
Desired action: [ACTION]
Language: [ENGLISH / SIMPLIFIED CHINESE / BILINGUAL]

Ask one question at a time if essential information is missing. Return the caption, suggested accessible visual direction, alt text, verification items and approval checks. Do not invent impact figures, quotations, hashtags, people or event details. Do not post it.
```

## 5. Prepare bilingual content

```text
Study the attached Metta brand package. Prepare an English and Simplified Chinese version of the attached approved content.

Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Channel: [CHANNEL]
Approved terminology or previous example: [ATTACH IT, OR I DO NOT KNOW]

Keep names, dates, eligibility, links and calls to action equivalent. Do not translate proper names or Metta terms from scratch when an approved term is unavailable. Mark uncertain wording [BILINGUAL REVIEW REQUIRED] and provide a side-by-side verification list. Human bilingual review is required before release.
```

## 6. Review something already created

```text
Study the attached Metta brand package and review the material I have attached. Do not publish it and do not rewrite it until after the review.

Tell me:
1. what is ready;
2. what must be corrected;
3. what facts or links must be verified;
4. whether the logo, language, accessibility, privacy and consent treatment is suitable; and
5. which approvals are still needed.

Classify each finding as Blocker, Required, Recommended or Optional. Then ask whether I want you to prepare a corrected draft.
```

## 7. Improve my rough prompt

```text
I am new to prompting. Study the attached Metta brand package. My rough request is:

[PASTE YOUR REQUEST]

Rewrite it as a clear Metta-safe prompt without changing my intended outcome. Show me the improved prompt first. Identify missing information, then ask me one simple question at a time. Do not create or publish the final material until the essential facts are available.
```

## Information you should prepare when available

- Purpose and intended audience
- Channel and dimensions
- Content owner and deadline
- Approved names, dates, venue, prices, eligibility and contacts
- Verified URLs, QR codes, forms and payment routes
- Required language and approved translations
- Official photographs, consent status and logo files
- Partner requirements and approving parties

Writing **I do not know** is acceptable. The correct result is a clearly marked question or `[TO CONFIRM]`, not an AI-generated guess.

## Final reminder

AI output is a draft. A person must verify the facts, language, links, accessibility, privacy, consent and approval route before anything is published, sent, printed or uploaded.
