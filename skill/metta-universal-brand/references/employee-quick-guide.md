# Metta Employee Quick Guide

Use this page for daily communication work. Refer to `METTA_UNIVERSAL_BRAND_GUIDE.md` when a task is sensitive, unusual or public-facing.

## Identity

- First public reference: **Metta Welfare Association (Metta)**.
- Chinese name in the official logo: **慈光福利协会**.
- Use the approved bilingual logo artwork; never reconstruct it.
- Corporate print colours: **Pantone 187 red** and **Pantone Blue 072**.
- Corporate English fonts: **Arial** and **Arial Narrow**.

## Write like Metta

- Be compassionate without pity.
- Use clear Singapore/British English.
- Respect dignity, choice, diversity and personal preferences.
- Use current service-specific terms such as client, resident, student, patient, care recipient, artist or participant.
- State facts plainly and identify anything that still requires verification.

## Do not

- Invent figures, testimonials, quotations, translations, dates, contacts or outcomes.
- Distort, recolour, crop, redraw or decorate the logo.
- Use guilt, fear or pressure in fundraising.
- Expose personal, medical, client or donor information.
- Publish, send or change a live system merely because AI produced a draft.

## Logo placement

There is no reviewed Metta rule saying the logo can never be placed at the bottom. A footer or bottom position is acceptable when the full logo is legible, has the required clear space and follows the approved template. Permanent signage, partner use and unusual placement require Community Partnership review.

## Universal AI prompt

```text
Use the Metta Universal Brand Guide to create or review this material. Use only the approved information I provide. Do not invent missing facts or alter the logo. Use respectful, accessible language. Return the complete draft, a verification list and the approvals required before release.
```

## Always confirm before release

- Names, date, time, venue, fees, eligibility and contacts
- URLs, QR codes, forms and payment routes
- English and Chinese equivalence
- Consent, privacy, safeguarding and copyright
- Accessibility and readable contrast
- Partner logo and fundraising disclosures
- Content-owner and Community Partnership approval where applicable
