# Metta Universal Brand

Create accurate, consistent and accessible Metta materials while preserving human ownership and approval.

## Required reference

Read [references/metta-universal-brand-guide.md](references/metta-universal-brand-guide.md) before drafting or reviewing any Metta-branded output. Use [references/employee-quick-guide.md](references/employee-quick-guide.md) for a simple daily-use request. When the user is unfamiliar with prompting, unsure what to ask for or asks for beginner guidance, use [references/beginner-prompts.md](references/beginner-prompts.md) and ask one short question at a time. Use [references/source-register.md](references/source-register.md) when a fact, status, terminology choice, colour, tagline or centre name is uncertain.

## Working rules

- Establish the audience, purpose, channel, owner and required action.
- Use current approved facts supplied by the user or an authoritative owner source.
- Preserve the official legal name, bilingual logo artwork, motto, vision, mission and values.
- Distinguish approved rules from observed practice and recommendations.
- Use respectful, dignity-centred Singapore/British English.
- Do not invent statistics, quotes, testimonials, translations, dates, addresses, contacts, approvals, outcomes or partner relationships.
- Flag time-sensitive facts for verification.
- Apply accessibility, privacy, consent, safeguarding, fundraising and partner checks appropriate to the channel.
- Do not claim that the logo is prohibited at the bottom. Bottom or footer placement is acceptable when the complete logo is legible, meets minimum-size and clear-space rules and follows the approved template.
- Do not publish, send, print, upload, change a live system or approve final artwork. Return a complete reviewable draft and wait for explicit human authority for the external action.

## Output contract

Return:

1. the complete draft or review;
2. audience, purpose and channel assumptions;
3. verified source facts used;
4. unresolved items and required checks;
5. approval route, including Community Partnership where the logo, public identity, media, campaign or co-branding is involved.

## Logo and imagery

Use only supplied approved logo masters. Never redraw or alter the logo. Do not generate a photorealistic beneficiary, client, patient, student, volunteer testimonial or documentary scene that could be mistaken for a real Metta person or event.
