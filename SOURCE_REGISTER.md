## Conflicts and open decisions

1. **Current tagline:** The 2008 manual formalises “Caring For Our Communities”; the 2024 handbook cover still shows it, but the body omits a tagline standard and newer communications emphasise other narrative lines. Confirm whether it remains an approved universal tagline.
2. **Digital colour values:** The approved handbook gives Pantone and CMYK, while current official vectors and the website contain several different hex values. Approve one digital palette for non-logo use.
3. **Chinese typeface:** The 2024 PDF text extraction corrupts the font name; the older manual identifies 文鼎中隶简. Confirm the licensed font file, usage rights and a modern fallback.
4. **Maitri status:** Current website and annual report show Maitri School activity; the volunteer handbook still describes a school/building opening in 2027. Confirm approved wording that separates the operating school from the future building.
5. **Centre count and directory:** Public sources use “ten centres” but programmes, sites and buildings are counted differently. Publish a controlled organisation directory with definitions and an owner.
6. **Terminology:** Current materials alternate among clients, beneficiaries, care recipients, residents, patients, students and trainees. Confirm service-level preferred terms while keeping person preference paramount.
7. **Chinese translations:** Confirm the controlled bilingual glossary for legal name, all centre/programme names, mission, values, service terms and calls to action.

## Evidence rule

When sources conflict, do not average or silently merge them. Record the conflict, use the most recent approved owner source where clear, and request confirmation when the decision could change public meaning, identity, safety, privacy, fundraising or governance.
