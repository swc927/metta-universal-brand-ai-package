## Logo placement

The Metta logo may appear at the top, bottom or another stable position when the approved template permits it. Keep the full bilingual composite legible, at least 20 mm wide in print, with the required clear space. Do not place it over busy imagery. Obtain Community Partnership approval for unusual or partner-led arrangements.
