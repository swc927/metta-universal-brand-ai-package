# Metta Communication Templates

Replace every `{{FIELD}}` with approved information. Remove unused fields before review. Do not ask AI to guess missing values.

- `communication-brief.md` - define the job before drafting.
- `email.md` - prepare a complete reviewable email.
- `social-media.md` - prepare accessible social content.
- `event-poster-brief.md` - brief a designer or AI without losing required information.
- `bilingual-announcement.md` - align English and Simplified Chinese content.
- `final-artwork-review.md` - complete the pre-release review.

Templates produce drafts only. Follow the approval route in the main guide before release.
